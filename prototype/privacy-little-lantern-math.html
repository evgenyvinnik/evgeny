Little Lantern Math

Privacy Policy

Effective 16 November 2026

In short. Little Lantern Math is a one-time purchase with no ads, no accounts and no tracking. It collects no information about you or your child and sends nothing to me or to anyone else.

Who this covers

This policy applies to Little Lantern Math (“the game”), a paid game for iPad that I publish on the App Store under the name Evgeny Vinnik. For this game it takes the place of the policy for my ad-supported apps. I am an independent developer and the person responsible for it. You can reach me at evgenyvinnik@gmail.com.

What the game collects

Nothing. The game has no ads, no analytics, no sign-up and no tracking, and no advertising or analytics code from other companies built into it. There is no server of mine for it to send anything to, and it never asks for permission to track you. That is why its App Store privacy label reads Data Not Collected.

Your progress stays on your iPad

Your progress, scores and settings are saved only on your iPad, where I cannot see them. They are removed when you delete the game. Like the rest of your iPad’s data, they may be included in your own iCloud or computer backups, which you and Apple control.

Buying the game

You buy the game once, through the App Store. Apple handles the payment, and I never receive your name, email address or payment details. Apple gives me sales reports that do not identify anyone. Your purchase is covered by Apple’s Privacy Policy.

Crash reports from Apple

If you have turned on Share with App Developers in Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements, Apple may pass me crash reports and usage statistics for the game, without anything that identifies you. I use them only to fix bugs and make the game run better. You can turn this off at any time in the same place.

Children

Little Lantern Math collects no personal information from anyone, so it collects none from children under 13, or under the minimum age set by the law where you live, either. There is nothing for a parent to consent to, review or delete. If you are a parent with a question about the game, email me and I will answer it.

Your rights

Privacy laws such as the GDPR in Europe and the CCPA in California give you the right to see, correct, delete or move your personal data, and to stop it being sold or shared. The game gives me no personal data, so there is nothing for me to show, export or delete, and nothing to sell or share. If you have a question about your data, email me and I will answer it.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. If the game ever starts collecting any data, this page will say so before that version of the game is released.

Questions about this policy or your data: evgenyvinnik@gmail.com.
