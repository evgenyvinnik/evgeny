Four years on a product with real customers and real deadlines. The team
went from five people to thirty around me.

I joined to write features and left having spent most of the last year on
the parts nobody demos: the deployment pipeline, the migration story, the
slow untangling of a data model that had been right for five customers and
wrong for five hundred.

The growth from five to thirty is the part I think about. Every process we
added solved a real problem and cost something we did not measure. Code
review caught bugs and slowed the obvious changes to a crawl.

What I would do differently: write the runbook before the feature, not after
the first incident.
